FAQ

Questions creators ask us.

Do I pay anything upfront?

No. We only earn when your product sells.

How much of my time does it take?

Very little. You review the draft and promote it. We do the rest.

How do I get paid?

We sell on Lynk.id, Shopee and TikTok Shop. You get a report of every sale each month.

Who owns the product?

You do. The product belongs to you, not to ThinkAct.

How long until launch?

At least 3–4 weeks from our first call.

My audience isn't in Indonesia. Can we still work together?

Yes. We work with creators worldwide and sell in USD.

Can I earn by introducing other creators to ThinkAct?

Yes. If you know a creator who should have their own product, you can earn a commission from ThinkAct's share when their product sells. It never comes out of the creator's share. See the partner program
